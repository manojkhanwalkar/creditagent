The service

CreditAgent shows you your own credit reports from the three US credit bureaus, a credit score computed by its own educational model, card offers, dark-web alerts and an automated assistant. The score is educational: lenders use their own scores, which may differ. Nothing here is financial, legal or credit advice, and offers are not firm offers of credit.

Your authorisation to obtain your credit reports

By ticking the box when you register, you give CreditAgent written instructions, under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, to obtain your consumer credit reports from the credit bureaus, now and while your account is open, to show them to you, compute your score and provide the features above. You can withdraw this authorisation at any time by deleting your account.
