Accessibility statement

Version 2026-11-14. How CreditAgent is made usable for people with disabilities.

Our commitment

CreditAgent aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, the standard used to judge accessibility under the Americans with Disabilities Act.

What we do

  • Every control has a visible label and works with a keyboard alone; focus is always visible.
  • Errors and status messages are announced to screen readers.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG contrast ratios, and pages work at 200% zoom and on narrow screens.
  • Before your session times out, we warn you and let you stay signed in.
  • Password fields accept paste and password managers.
  • Sign-in codes can go by email or text message, whichever suits you; the code field accepts paste and your device's one-time-code autofill, and you can ask for a new code.
  • Every change to the interface is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA before it is released.

Known limitations

Registration needs a photo of your Social Security card. If you cannot take or upload one, contact us and we will help you register another way.

Feedback

If something is hard to use with your assistive technology, tell us at accessibility@creditagent.example . We aim to reply within 5 business days.
