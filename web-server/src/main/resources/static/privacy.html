Privacy notice

Version 2026-11-18. How CreditAgent collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information.

What we collect

  • Account details: your user id, email address, ZIP code and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Mobile number: used only to text you one-time sign-in codes, never for marketing.
  • Sign-in codes: each one-time code is kept only as a keyed hash, for at most 10 minutes.
  • Your Social Security number: read by our identity provider from the photo of your card. We keep only a keyed hash of it and its last four digits; the photo is not stored.
  • Credit information: your reports from the three credit bureaus, stored encrypted, and the latest score we computed from them.
  • Dark-web alerts: where and when your email address was found exposed.
  • Usage records: sign-in times, and an audit record each time your credit report is read or its lock changed, kept to protect your account and investigate misuse.
  • Chat questions: kept in memory for at most 5 minutes; a redacted copy is kept for up to 30 days to test the assistant's safety checks.

We do not use cookies or tracking technologies for advertising or analytics.

Why we use it

To verify your identity, show you your credit reports, score, card offers and alerts, answer your questions, keep your account secure, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not use it to make credit decisions about you.

Who receives it

  • Our identity-verification provider (the photo of your card, to read and verify your SSN).
  • The credit bureaus (to obtain your reports, on your authorisation).
  • Our email provider (to send your alerts, and the sign-in codes you ask for by email).
  • Our text-message provider and the mobile carriers (your mobile number and the sign-in code, when you ask for a code by text message).
  • If you connect CreditAgent to Claude, the Claude app you authorise receives your score, report and advice in your conversations. An optional AI model that writes advice receives only de-identified report data.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act we do not share your nonpublic personal information with non-affiliated third parties except as described here and as the law permits, so there is nothing to opt out of.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Accounts with no sign-in (on the site or through a connected Claude app) for two years are deleted with their data. Stored credit reports nobody has viewed or refreshed for two years are deleted. Dark-web alerts are deleted two years after they are found. Security audit records (when your report was read, when your account changed) are kept for at least one year, also after you delete your account, to protect accounts and investigate misuse.

Your rights and choices

  • Download your data: signed in, use “Download my data” in the “Your data” panel.
  • Delete your account and data: use “Delete my account” in the same panel.
  • Lock your credit report so third parties cannot pull it.
  • California and other state residents may also ask to know, correct or delete their information; we do not sell or share it, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

Credit reports are encrypted, your SSN is never stored in readable form, passwords are hashed, every sign-in needs a one-time code sent to your email or mobile phone as well as your password, and sessions end after 5 idle minutes.
